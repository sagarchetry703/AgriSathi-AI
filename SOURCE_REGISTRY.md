# 📚 AgriSathi AI — Source & Knowledge Registry

This document records the official, verified regional agricultural, meteorological, and disaster management sources powering AgriSathi AI.

---

## 1. Agricultural Science & Crop Varieties
- **Assam Agricultural University (AAU), Jorhat**:
  - *Package of Practices for Kharif and Rabi Crops of Assam*.
  - Recommended flood-submergence tolerant rice varieties: **Swarna Sub-1**, **Bahadur Sub-1**, **Ranjit Sub-1**, **Jalashree**, **Jalkuwari**.
- **ICAR - Central Rice Research Institute (CRRI)**: Deepwater Bao rice cultivation practices in flood plains.
- **Tocklai Tea Research Institute (TRA), Jorhat**: Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) for Assam Tea plantations, red spider mite, looper caterpillar, and blister blight control.
- **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Network, Assam**: District-level advisories for Kamrup, Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Barpeta, Nagaon, and Cachar.

---

## 2. Weather & Climate Data
- **Open-Meteo Weather Forecast API**:
  - Global Open Data Weather API, WMO-compliant.
  - Parameters: 2m Temperature, Relative Humidity, Precipitation, Wind Speed, WMO Weather Codes.
  - Coordinate Precision: 10 verified Assam administrative centers (Guwahati, Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Silchar, Tezpur, Nagaon, Barpeta, Sivasagar, Tinsukia, Dhubri).

## 3. Disaster Management & Flood Advisory
- **Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA)**:
  - Official Web Portal: https://asdma.assam.gov.in/
  - State Emergency Operation Centre: Toll Free 1070 / 1079
  - Flood Early Warning & Emergency Kits guidelines.
- **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)**:
  - Sachet Disaster Alert Portal: https://sachet.ndma.gov.in/
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD) - Regional Meteorological Centre, Guwahati**:
  - District-level agromet advisory bulletins.

## 4. Market Prices (Mandi)
- **AGMARKNET / eNAM (Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare)**:
  - Official Portal: https://agmarknet.gov.in/
  - Policy: In compliance with project ethics, when a verified live API token is not provisioned, AgriSathi explicitly marks live mandi data as "Unavailable" rather than fabricating numbers.
